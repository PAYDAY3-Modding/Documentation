== Create a Project

In the Audiokinetic Launcher:

. Go to the Wwise page
. Find the Installed Version called "2023.1.15.8789" and click on its "Launch Wwise (64-bit)" button
. When Wwise launches, click on "New..."
. In the name section, put in a name for the project
. (Optional) Change Location and Project Folder if needed
. Click on "OK"

You have created a Wwise project!

[IMPORTANT]
====
You will most likely get a popup talking about no valid license key being found. This is harmless unless you make a lot of Wwise stuff. Just click close on the popup and it'll go away.
====

== Setting up Project for Modkit

While in the Wwise project:

. In the top bar, click on "Project", then "Project Settings..."
. Click on the "SoundBanks" tab

You should see a bunch of things enabled and disabled. Change the settings so that the following is enabled:

. "Enable Auto-Defined SoundBanks"
. "Copy Loose/Streamed Media" 
. "Remove Unused Generated Files" 
. "Use SoundBank Names for Filenames" 
. "Generate Per Bank Metadata File"
. "Generate XML Metadata"
. "Generate JSON Metadata"
. Everything under "Include in Metadata"

Everything else you can leave as their default state.

Now, after changing the settings of the Wwise project and still being inside it:

. Hold down Shift & B. This will open up the SoundBank Manager
. Click on "Generate All"

This will generate a blank Soundbank along with the "GeneratedSoundBanks" folder.

== Connecting Project to Modkit

This is the easiest part if you know what you're doing, or hardest if you don't know.

. Open the Modkit
. Click on "Edit" in the top bar, then "Project Settings..."
. Scroll down on the far left to the bottom
. Click on "Integration Settings" under the "Wwise" catagory
. In the "Wwise Project Path" part, put in the path to your project + the .wproj file of the project (which should be at "WwiseProjects/(PROJECTNAME)/PROJECTNAME.wproj
. In the "Root Output Path", put the path of the "GeneratedSoundBanks" folder (which should be at "WwiseProjects/(PROJECTNAME)/GeneratedSoundbanks"
. Finally, in the editor top bar, click on "Window", then "Wwise Browser", then click on "Generate SoundBanks..."

Congrats, you've connected the modkit to your Wwise project!
