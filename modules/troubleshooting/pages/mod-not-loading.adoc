= Mod Does Not Load

If a forged CrimeFactory mod does not load, verify the runtime layout before debugging the mod itself.

. Confirm the Forge completed successfully.
. Confirm the complete mod folder is under `PAYDAY3/Mods/`.
. Confirm the mod's `.uplugin` file is present in that folder.
. Confirm PDML initializes successfully.
. Confirm the mod's required dependencies are installed.
. Confirm the modkit and game versions are compatible.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Do not move a normal PDML mod into `PAYDAY3/Content/Paks/` as a troubleshooting step.

PDML expects CrimeFactory plugin mods under the dedicated `PAYDAY3/Mods/` directory.
====
