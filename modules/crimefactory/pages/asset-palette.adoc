= Asset Palette

The Asset Palette is a dockable CrimeFactory window for finding assets to use in custom heists. It groups available heist assets into category tabs, making it easier to see what you can use and access the assets you need.

== Open the Asset Palette

In the editor's top toolbar, select *Window > CrimeFactory > Asset Palette*.

You can dock the window alongside your other editor panels to keep it available while working on your heist.

image::asset-palette.png[CrimeFactory Asset Palette with the Doors category selected, category tabs and a search bar above a grid of asset previews, width=557]

== Browse assets

Select a category tab to browse its assets. Each entry displays a preview and an asset name, so you can identify what you need without searching through the game's folders.

The categories shown above include:

* *Doors*
* *Vehicles*
* *Volumes*
* *Objective Items*

Scroll through the selected category to see more entries.

== Search within a category

. Select the category you want to search.
. Enter an asset name or part of its name in *Search this category...* at the top of the window.
. Clear the search text to browse the full category again.

The search applies to the selected category. Switch tabs to look for an asset in another category.

== New assets and categories

The Asset Palette will be updated as the game and modkit change, with more assets and categories added over time. The available entries may differ from the screenshot depending on your modkit version.
