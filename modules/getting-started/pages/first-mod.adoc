= Create Your First Mod
:description: Replace the PAYDAY 3 title and main-menu logos with your own textures, then Forge and test your first CrimeFactory mod.

== What we are going to make

We will make a texture replacement mod that changes the PAYDAY 3 logos shown on the title screen and in the main menu. Once you Forge the mod and load the game, you can see your replacement artwork immediately without entering a heist.

We will replace these two textures:

[source,text]
----
PAYDAY3/Content/UI/Textures/Generic/SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02
PAYDAY3/Content/UI/Textures/Generic/Tex_Payday_3_Logo
----

CrimeFactory will create the mod as an Unreal plugin: a folder that keeps the mod's assets and information together. PDML loads the finished mod when the game starts. We will use `MyFirstMod` as its name throughout the tutorial.

By the end, you will have:

* Created a Blank mod with CrimeFactory.
* Found both logo textures and converted them to editable uncooked assets.
* Reimported your own images into those assets.
* Added both replacements to the mod's PAL so Forge includes them.
* Forged and installed the mod, then checked the logos in PAYDAY 3.

== Before you start

Complete xref:project-setup.adoc[Set Up the Project] and xref:editor-tour.adoc[Editor Tour]. You should have the modkit open in the custom PAYDAY 3 editor, with CrimeFactory available and the game path configured.

You also need:

* The cooked PAYDAY 3 assets visible in the Content Browser. See xref:modkit:cooked-assets.adoc[Cooked Assets] if they are missing.
* Your replacement artwork saved as image files, such as PNGs. You can use the same image for both logos or prepare a different image for each.
* PDML installed for the in-game test. See xref:install-test.adoc[Install and Test] for the runtime prerequisite.

Choose artwork that is easy to distinguish from the original logo. When preparing each image, use the original texture's dimensions as a starting point and preserve transparency if you want the artwork to appear without a rectangular background.

== Create a Blank CrimeFactory mod

CrimeFactory creates the plugin's folders and files for you, including a content folder and mod information. Forge prepares these files for use in the game.

Choose the *Blank* template for this mod. It creates the plugin and standard CrimeFactory assets, including `PAL_<ModName>` and `DA_<ModName>`. The PAL, or Primary Asset Label, tells CrimeFactory which assets to package. We will add both logo replacements to it later.

=== Open Create Mod

Open CrimeFactory and click *Create Mod* to open the *New Mod* window.

image::crimefactory:CrimeFactory-new-mod-window.png[CrimeFactory New Mod window, width=1000]

=== Choose the template and name

. Select the *Blank* template.
. Enter `MyFirstMod` in *Mod Name*.
. Fill in *Author* with your name and *Description* with a short description, such as `Replaces the title and main-menu logos with my own artwork.`
. Use the normal development location, `<ModkitRoot>/Mods/`, where CrimeFactory creates mod plugins.
. Click *Create Mod* and let CrimeFactory generate the plugin.

See xref:crimefactory:templates.adoc[Templates] for the other starting templates and xref:crimefactory:create-mod.adoc[Create Mod] for the full creation-window reference.

[CAUTION]
====
Keep the plugin name stable once you start creating referenced assets. The name becomes part of its mount point and asset paths; renaming it later can break references.
====

=== Find the generated assets

. Find `MyFirstMod` in the CrimeFactory mod manager.
. Click the mod's icon to open its content directory in the Content Browser.
. Locate `PAL_MyFirstMod`. You will open this asset after editing the textures.

Keep the files and structure generated by CrimeFactory together. The PAL stays in the mod's content directory, while the logo replacements will stay at their original game paths.

== Find the two logo textures

In the editor, `PAYDAY3/Content/` corresponds to the `/Game/` content root. The two textures therefore have these package paths:

[source,text]
----
/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02
/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/Tex_Payday_3_Logo
----

. Open the Content Browser and navigate to the game's `UI/Textures/Generic` folder under `/Game/`.
. Search for `SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02` and locate the texture.
. Open it to inspect the original logo and note its dimensions, then close the texture editor.
. Locate `Tex_Payday_3_Logo` in the same folder and inspect it too. Clear the previous search or change the search text to find the second texture.

The original cooked assets open in read-only mode. Convert them before trying to change their image data.

== Convert the textures to uncooked assets

. Right-click `SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02` in the Content Browser and select *Convert to Uncooked*.
. Let the conversion finish.
. Right-click `Tex_Payday_3_Logo` and select *Convert to Uncooked* too.

image::getting-started:Cooked-Texture.png[Cooked asset context menu showing Convert to Uncooked, width=1100]

*Convert to Uncooked* creates an editable version at the same `/Game/...` path. The editor uses this version in place of the read-only game asset. Your installed game's archive files (IoStore files) stay unchanged.

[IMPORTANT]
====
Keep both textures at their original names and paths. For this direct replacement, use *Convert to Uncooked* and leave the resulting assets in `/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/`.

Moving them into `MyFirstMod`'s content directory or importing new textures there would give them different paths. The game needs the replacements at the paths it already uses for its logos.
====

See xref:creating-mods:asset-replacements.adoc[Asset Replacements] for the full conversion and replacement workflow.

== Reimport your replacement artwork

Repeat these steps for each uncooked texture:

. Double-click the uncooked texture in the Content Browser to open its texture editor.
. Click *Reimport* and choose the image file you want to use as its replacement.
. Check the texture preview to confirm that it now displays your artwork.
. Save the texture.

Do this for both `SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02` and `Tex_Payday_3_Logo`. Each texture should now preview the image you selected for it.

Reimporting updates the image data in the existing texture asset, keeping the asset name and path that the game references. If you revise the artwork later, reimport it again, save the texture, and Forge the mod again before testing.

== Add both replacements to the mod's PAL

The edited textures are outside `MyFirstMod`'s content folder. Add each one to the PAL so CrimeFactory knows to package it with your mod.

Follow the xref:creating-mods:asset-replacements.adoc#_add_the_replacement_to_the_pal[Add the replacement to the PAL] step for both textures:

. Return to `MyFirstMod` in the CrimeFactory manager and click its icon to open the mod's content directory.
. Open `PAL_MyFirstMod`.
. Find and expand *Explicit Assets*.
. Add an array entry and use its asset picker to select the modified `SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02` texture at `/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02`.
. Add another entry and select the modified `Tex_Payday_3_Logo` texture at `/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/Tex_Payday_3_Logo`.
. Save `PAL_MyFirstMod`.

image::getting-started:Example-PAL.png[Example PAL, width=1100]

The PAL's *Explicit Assets* list should include both:

[source,text]
----
/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/SS_UI_PD3_Logo_02
/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/Tex_Payday_3_Logo
----

[IMPORTANT]
====
Saving the textures alone does not tell CrimeFactory to include them. Both modified uncooked assets must be listed in `PAL_MyFirstMod`'s *Explicit Assets* array.
====

Check the PAL's *Chunk ID* as well. It must avoid the game's and DLC's chunk IDs; a value greater than `100` is recommended. See xref:crimefactory:pal.adoc[PAL & Explicit Assets] for the packaging settings.

== Forge and install the mod

. Save both textures and `PAL_MyFirstMod`.
. Validate `MyFirstMod` in CrimeFactory and resolve any blocking errors.
. Click *FORGE* on `MyFirstMod` and wait for a successful result. CrimeFactory uses the PAL to include the external `/Game/...` replacements alongside the plugin content.
. If *Copy Mods To Game* is enabled in CrimeFactory Project Settings, the successful Forge copies the mod to the game. Otherwise, copy the complete forged `MyFirstMod` folder from the output location shown by CrimeFactory into `PAYDAY3/Mods/`.

The installed mod should be at `PAYDAY3/Mods/MyFirstMod/`, with its `.uplugin` file and all packaged content kept together.

The development plugin under `<ModkitRoot>/Mods/` is your working source. Install the forged output into the game.

See xref:package-mod.adoc[Forge the Mod] and xref:install-test.adoc[Install and Test] for more detail on packaging and installation.

== Check the result in PAYDAY 3

. Launch PAYDAY 3 with PDML installed.
. Verify that PDML discovers and loads `MyFirstMod`.
. Check the title screen: the PAYDAY 3 title logo should now display your replacement artwork.
. Continue to the main menu and check its logo too.

Replacing both textures covers the title-screen and main-menu logos. You should see the images you reimported as soon as you reach those screens.

=== If a logo is still unchanged

Check that:

* Both uncooked textures remain at the exact original `/Game/UI/Textures/Generic/` paths with their original names.
* Each texture previews your replacement artwork and has been saved.
* Both textures are listed in `PAL_MyFirstMod`'s *Explicit Assets*, and the PAL has been saved.
* You Forged again after the latest changes and installed the latest complete forged folder.
* PDML loaded `MyFirstMod` when the game started.

If only one logo changed, check the other texture's preview and PAL entry. After correcting anything, save, Forge again, update the installed mod, and restart PAYDAY 3.
