== What you will do

By the end of the guide, you will understand the toolchain, open the PAYDAY 3 modding project, create a plugin mod with CrimeFactory, Forge it, install it under `PAYDAY3/Mods/`, and test it with PAYDAY 3.

You do not need to understand PAYDAY 3 reverse engineering or Unreal Engine internals before starting.
