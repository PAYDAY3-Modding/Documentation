= Custom Heists

CrimeFactory includes a custom-heist template and PAYDAY 3-specific tooling for authoring new heists.

This area is more advanced than a basic mod. Complete xref:getting-started:index.adoc[Getting Started] first.

This section assumes you know the basics of Unreal Engine, such as:

- Levels and sublevels
- Level Blueprints
- Volumes
- Replication
- Cinematics
- and more

[IMPORTANT]
====
Custom-heist support is not uniform across every PAYDAY 3 subsystem.

Individual pages identify supported, experimental, in-progress, and known-limitation areas.
====
